# AI Companion Usage & Dependency Analysis

### Exploring how AI companion usage relates to social interaction and psychological wellbeing.

## About the Project

AI companions are becoming a part of everyday life, whether people use them for learning, productivity, entertainment, or companionship.

I wanted to explore how people use these tools and whether their usage patterns are associated with changes in social interaction and wellbeing indicators.

For this project, I worked with a dataset of 1,000 simulated survey records. I used Python to clean and explore the data, identify patterns, and study relationships between different variables. I then built an interactive Power BI dashboard to present the findings in a way that is easier to understand.

## What I Explored

- How much time people spend interacting with AI companions.
- Which AI platforms and use cases are most common in the dataset.
- How AI usage relates to loneliness and emotional attachment scores.
- The relationship between AI usage and human social interaction.
- Associations between AI usage and sleep quality, anxiety, depression, and self-esteem.
- Differences in wellbeing scores across dependency-risk categories.

## Project Workflow

**1. Data Understanding & Cleaning**

Started by exploring the dataset, checking its structure, identifying missing values and duplicates, and reviewing data consistency.

**2. Exploratory Data Analysis**

Used Python to understand distributions, compare different variables, calculate descriptive statistics, and explore patterns in AI companion usage.

**3. Correlation Analysis**

Examined how daily AI chat hours relate to loneliness, emotional attachment, human social interaction, sleep quality, and other wellbeing indicators.

**4. Dashboard Development**

Built a two-page Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, scatter plots, comparison charts, and interactive slicers. The slicers allow users to explore the data by AI platform and primary use case.

## Some Interesting Findings

A few relationships stood out during the analysis:

| Variables | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| AI usage & loneliness | +0.744 |
| AI usage & emotional attachment | +0.748 |
| AI usage & human social interaction | -0.620 |
| AI usage & sleep quality | -0.707 |

These results show interesting patterns within the dataset and formed the basis for further exploration in the Power BI dashboard.

## Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard is divided into two sections:

**Page 1 – AI Usage Overview**

Explores dependency-risk label distribution, AI usage across platforms and use cases, and relationships between AI usage, loneliness, and human social interaction.

**Page 2 – Wellbeing & Dependency Analysis**

Focuses on emotional attachment, sleep quality, anxiety, depression, and self-esteem, including comparisons across dependency-risk categories.
